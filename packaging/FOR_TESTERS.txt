Meeting Assistant - test build
==============================

Thanks for trying it! Everything runs on your own computer and is free. Your recordings
never leave it.

What you need
- Windows 10 or 11
- About 7 GB of free disk space (the AI models)
- 8 GB of RAM or more works best. With less, pick the lighter AI model during setup.

Install
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you keep programs, for example Documents\MeetingAssistant.
   Keep all the files together; MeetingAssistant.exe needs the folder next to it.
2. Double-click MeetingAssistant.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's because the app isn't signed with a
   paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. A setup window walks you through the rest:
   - Install Ollama (free, from ollama.com) when it asks. It runs the AI locally.
   - Download the AI model and the speech model. This takes a while, once.

Try it
- Start recording in a call (or play a YouTube video of a meeting), type a few notes,
  stop, then press Process meeting.
- Note shortcuts: "!" must be in the summary, "internal:" never goes into client emails,
  "fr:" marks a feature request. Ctrl+Shift+Space adds a note from any app.
- Processing a 30-minute meeting takes a few minutes, longer on laptops without a graphics card.

If something breaks
- Error details are saved in: %USERPROFILE%\MeetingAssistant\logs\crash.log
  Please send that file back along with what you were doing.
- Your meetings are in %USERPROFILE%\MeetingAssistant. Delete that folder to start fresh.

Gmail and Notion are optional and need their own one-time setup (see Settings in the app).
